What we offer

No upselling. No packages. What you need, placed precisely.

Botox can be used for two categories of treatment: therapeutic and aesthetic. Therapeutic Botox targets the masseter muscle to reduce jaw clenching, the temporalis for tension headaches, and the mentalis for chin dimpling related to jaw dysfunction. Aesthetic Botox addresses frown lines (glabella), forehead lines, and crow's feet, placed conservatively to maintain natural expression.

Every plan begins with a consultation to understand the concern, the relevant anatomy, and the result you want. Treatment is designed around careful dosing and precise placement, without packages or unnecessary add-ons.

Questions

  • Is this a medspa?

    No. This is a clinical practice, and Botox is approached conservatively with careful assessment and precise placement. The goal is an appropriate result, not volume sales.

  • How long does Botox last for jaw tension?

    For therapeutic jaw applications (masseter, temporalis), Botox typically lasts 3 to 6 months. The effect on grinding and headache frequency is usually noticeable within 2 weeks of injection. Many patients repeat every 4 to 5 months.

  • Do I need to come in for a consultation first?

    A consultation is always the first step. When the treatment area and dose are clear, the consultation and Botox treatment can often happen in the same appointment. Book a consultation and we'll determine the right approach.

  • Will I look overdone?

    Not the goal. The approach is conservative, enough to make a visible difference, not so much that the result reads as injected. If you've had experiences with overdone results elsewhere, describe what you didn't like. The consultation is a conversation, not a sales pitch.
