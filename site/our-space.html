The clinic

Curtained treatment bays. One clinician per patient. Undivided attention for the full hour.

Suite 1007 at 16 West 32nd Street is a quiet floor in NoMad. Treatment happens in curtained bays, one clinician per patient. When you're in a session, the clinician is with you, focused on the work, for the full sixty minutes.

The space was built around the clinical model, not the other way around. Sixty-minute sessions require a setup that supports sixty minutes of continuous, focused work. Manual therapy tables, ultrasound, e-stim, the tools are there. The distractions aren't.
